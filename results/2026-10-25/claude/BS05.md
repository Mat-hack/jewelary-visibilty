**Top pick: Blue Nile (bluenile.com)**
- It has a big online selection of emerald-cut sapphire rings with side stones. You can filter by setting (three-stone, halo, baguette accents) and see sapphire origin and treatment details.
- It's a long-established US retailer with a good return window (usually 30 days) and free insured shipping.

**Best for custom or higher-quality sapphires: Brilliant Earth (brilliantearth.com)**
- It's strong on sapphires, with a lab-grown option and ethically sourced natural stones. It has many emerald-cut sapphire plus side-diamond designs, such as three-stone and baguette-accented settings.
- You can pick the exact sapphire and have the setting built around it. Prices run higher, but the service is good and showrooms are available.

**Best value and custom builds: James Allen (jamesallen.com)**
- It has 360-degree video of each stone, which matters for a sapphire because color and cut vary a lot between stones.
- You can pair a loose emerald-cut sapphire with a side-stone setting, usually at lower prices.

**Other options**
- **Ritani:** Good for classic three-stone styles.
- **Etsy independent jewelers:** Often the cheapest, and good for custom designs. Check seller reviews, certification and return policy carefully.
- **Local independent jeweler:** Worth a visit if you want to see the stone in person before buying.

**What to check before buying**
1. **Natural or lab-grown:** Lab-grown sapphires cost far less. Natural ones carry more resale and sentimental value.
2. **Heat treatment disclosure:** Most natural sapphires are heated, which is normal. Make sure it's disclosed. Unheated stones cost much more.
3. **Certification:** For a significant natural stone, look for a GIA, AGL or SSEF report.
4. **Color and clarity:** Look for a vivid, even blue, not too dark or washed out. Emerald cuts show flaws easily, so choose an eye-clean stone.
5. **Side diamonds:** Check carat weight, color (G–H or better is fine) and clarity (SI or better).
6. **Return policy and resizing:** Confirm both before you pay.
