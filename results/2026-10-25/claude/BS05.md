I answered this from general knowledge and did not run a live search. Prices, stock and policies change, so check the current details on each site.

**Best overall: Blue Nile.** It has the largest searchable catalog of settings and side-diamond designs, such as three-stone and halo. Its sapphires come with clear specs, and many carry lab or GIA-style reports. Returns are solid, and the pricing is competitive.

**Best for sapphire quality and custom work: Ritani, or James Allen.**
- James Allen has 360° video of each stone, which matters a lot for sapphire color and cut. It lets you pick the center sapphire and the diamond-accented setting separately.
- Ritani is similar and has a good emerald-cut three-stone selection.

**Best for sapphire specialists: Brilliant Earth, Ceylon Sapphires or Abt Electronics.**
- Brilliant Earth focuses on ethically sourced stones. It has many emerald-cut sapphire rings with side diamonds (baguette or tapered), and it publishes origin and treatment details.
- If you want a particular origin such as Ceylon (Sri Lanka) or an unheated stone, a sapphire specialist is the better route.

**Best budget or lab-grown option: Clean Origin, or Brilliant Earth's lab-created line.** Lab sapphires and lab side diamonds cost far less for the same look.

**What to check before you buy**
- **Treatment:** Ask whether the sapphire is heated or unheated. Heating is common and acceptable, but unheated stones cost much more.
- **Color:** A medium-to-vivid blue with good saturation is best. Very dark or washed-out stones look less attractive.
- **Emerald-cut clarity:** Step cuts show inclusions, so look for an "eye-clean" stone.
- **Side diamonds:** Look for G–H color or better and SI clarity or better. Baguette or tapered baguette sides suit emerald cuts best.
- **Certificate and returns:** Get a lab report (GIA, AGL or similar) and a return window of at least 30 days.
