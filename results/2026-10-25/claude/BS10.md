Best places to buy a 14k gold gemstone solitaire pendant in the USA, by what you care about. This is from general knowledge and I did not check live prices or stock, so confirm current policies before you buy.

**Best overall for quality and value: Blue Nile or James Allen (same parent company)**
- Large online selection, with detailed photos and gemstone specs.
- Strong return windows, usually 30 days.
- Gemstone selection is more limited than their diamond selection. Check that the stone is graded or described in detail.

**Best for custom or higher-end colored gemstones: Brilliant Earth or Ritani**
- Good ethical sourcing information, with sapphires and other colored stones in 14k gold settings.
- Prices are higher than at the discount sellers below.

**Best for budget and variety: Amazon (sold by reputable jewelers), Etsy, or Zales/Kay**
- Etsy: many independent makers, and you can request custom stone and setting combinations. Check seller reviews and make sure "14k" is stated and stamped.
- Zales and Kay: easy to shop in person and good for gifts. Lab-created or treated stones are common at these price points.

**Best for lab-created stones: Ana Luisa, Mejuri, or Kay's lab collections**
- Ana Luisa and Mejuri sell trendy minimalist solitaire pendants. Mejuri offers solid 14k gold and good packaging.
- If you want natural stones, confirm that before ordering.

**Best for big savings: Costco**
- Occasional gemstone pendants with a certificate and a generous return policy.

**What to check before you buy**
1. **Solid 14k, not plated or filled.** Look for a "14k" or "585" stamp and the words "solid gold" in the listing.
2. **Natural, lab-created, or treated.** Many sapphires, rubies, and emeralds are heat-treated or oil-treated. Reputable sellers disclose this.
3. **Stone details.** Look for carat weight, dimensions, and cut quality. Ask for a certificate for any stone over about 1 carat.
4. **Chain.** Confirm the chain is also 14k, and check its length and thickness.
5. **Return policy.** Look for at least 30 days.
