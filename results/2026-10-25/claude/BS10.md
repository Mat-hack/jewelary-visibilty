**Best overall picks for a 14k gold gemstone solitaire pendant**

1. **Blue Nile** is best for quality and certification. It has a large selection of gemstone pendants, clear 14k gold specs, and a good return policy. It's a good fit if you want sapphire, ruby or emerald with detailed grading information.
2. **Brilliant Earth** is best for ethically sourced stones. It offers sapphire and other gemstone solitaires in 14k gold, and its designs are modern. Prices are higher.
3. **James Allen** is best for seeing the exact stone. It has 360° imaging and lets you choose your own setting.
4. **Mejuri** (14k solid gold) and **Catbird** are best for delicate, everyday pieces at mid-range prices.
5. **Amazon** and **Etsy** are best for budget and custom options.
   - Amazon sells brands like Amazon Collection and Gem Stone King. Check that the listing says "14k solid gold" and not "gold-plated" or "gold-filled".
   - On Etsy, buy only from sellers with strong reviews and a clear return policy.
6. **Costco** is worth a look for lab-created or natural gemstone pendants at good prices, with a generous return policy.
7. **Local independent jewelers** are best if you want to see the stone in person, get a custom setting, or have service after the sale.

**What to check before buying**
- **Gold marking:** Look for "14k" or "585" stamped on the piece, and confirm it's solid gold, not plated.
- **Natural or lab-created:** Lab-created stones cost much less. Make sure the listing says which one it is.
- **Stone quality:** Look at the carat weight, color, clarity and cut. For colored stones, color matters most.
- **Treatments:** Sapphires and emeralds are often heated or oiled. Reputable sellers disclose this.
- **Certification:** For stones over about 1 ct, ask for a GIA or AGL report.
- **Chain:** Check that the chain is also 14k gold and a suitable length (16-18 inches is typical).
- **Returns:** Look for a return window of 30 days or more.
