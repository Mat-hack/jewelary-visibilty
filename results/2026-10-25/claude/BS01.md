**What to check before buying**
- **Natural or lab-created:** Lab-created sapphires cost much less. Natural stones should come with a grading report (GIA or AGL, for example).
- **Treatment:** Most natural sapphires are heat-treated. Make sure the listing says so.
- **Metal and backs:** 14k or 18k gold or platinum is best for sensitive ears. Screw backs are more secure than friction backs.
- **Carat weight:** Check whether the listed weight is per earring or the total for the pair.
- **Return policy:** Look for at least 30 days.
