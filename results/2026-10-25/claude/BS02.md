There's no single best place. The right retailer depends on your budget and on whether you want natural or lab-grown rubies. I answered from general knowledge and didn't check live prices or stock, so confirm current pricing and return policies before you buy.

**Best overall for quality and selection: Blue Nile or James Allen**
- Both are established online jewelers with detailed listings, certified diamonds, and good photos and video.
- Both usually offer 30-day returns.
- Search "ruby diamond halo studs" to see a wide range of metals and sizes.

**Best value: Brilliant Earth, Ritani, or Costco**
- Brilliant Earth focuses on ethically sourced stones and has good return and warranty terms.
- Costco often has competitive prices on 14k gold halo studs with a generous return policy, if you're a member.

**Budget or fashion-grade: Amazon, Zales, Kay, or Macy's**
- These carry many options, often with lab-created or treated rubies and small diamond accents.
- Read the stone descriptions closely. "Created" and "lab-grown" mean something different from natural. Many low-priced rubies are also heat-treated or glass-filled, so look for the treatment to be disclosed.
- On Amazon, buy only from sellers with strong reviews and a clear return policy.

**Best for unique or vintage pieces: Etsy or 1stDibs**
- Etsy suits independent designers, and 1stDibs has higher-end antique and vintage pieces.
- Check the seller's reviews, return policy, and whether they provide appraisals.

**What to check before buying**
- **Ruby type:** natural (often heat-treated), lab-grown, or glass-filled. Lab-grown rubies cost much less and look identical.
- **Metal:** 14k or 18k gold or platinum. Avoid gold-plated or vermeil if you want durability.
- **Diamonds:** the halo stones are usually small melee, so a G-H color and SI clarity is fine at that size.
- **Backs:** screw-backs or locking friction backs are safer than basic pushbacks.
- **Returns:** look for 30 days or more, and ask for documentation of stone origin and treatment.
