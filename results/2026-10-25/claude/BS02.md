There's no single best place. The right retailer depends on your budget and on whether you want natural or lab-grown stones. This comes from general knowledge, not live research, so check current prices and policies before you buy.

**Best overall for quality and selection: Blue Nile or James Allen**
- Both are online retailers with large catalogs of ruby and diamond halo studs.
- They provide grading details and good photos or 360° video.
- Both have solid return windows, usually about 30 days.

**Best for custom or higher-end pieces: Brilliant Earth, Ritani, or a local independent jeweler**
- A jeweler can match the ruby color and size to your taste.
- Brilliant Earth also emphasizes ethical sourcing.

**Best for budget: Amazon (reputable sellers), Costco, or Kay/Zales**
- Costco has good return policies and often fair prices on certified stones.
- Mall chains like Kay and Zales often discount heavily. Compare prices before buying, because the sticker prices are high.
- Amazon is where you need to be most careful about the seller.

**Best for lab-grown stones: Brilliant Earth, Clean Origin, or Blue Nile's lab-grown line**
- Lab-grown rubies and diamonds cost much less than natural ones and look identical.

**What to check before buying**
- **Ruby quality:** Many rubies are heat-treated, which is normal. Heavily fracture-filled or glass-filled rubies are much less valuable. Ask for the treatment to be disclosed.
- **Certification:** Look for GIA, AGL, or IGI documentation where possible. Smaller studs may not have a certificate, but the seller should still state the specs.
- **Metal:** 14k gold is a good balance of durability and price. Platinum costs more.
- **Settings:** For studs, choose secure backs such as screw-backs or friction backs with locking discs.
- **Return policy:** Make sure you can return them. Look for 30 days or more.
