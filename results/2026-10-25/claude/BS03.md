I didn't search live listings, so I can't confirm current stock or prices. These are the places that usually carry an oval blue sapphire pendant with a diamond halo:

**Online jewelers (large selections, usually with certified stones)**
- Blue Nile: lab-grown and natural sapphire pendants, with filters for shape and halo setting.
- James Allen: sapphire and diamond halo pendants, with 360-degree views.
- Brilliant Earth: ethically sourced sapphires, including custom options.
- Kay, Zales, Jared and Signet brands: a wide range at mid prices, with in-store pickup.
- Ross-Simons and Amazon (sold by reputable jewelers): lower prices, mostly sapphires that are heat-treated or lab-created.
- Etsy: handmade and custom pieces from independent jewelers. Check seller reviews and ask for a gemstone certificate.

**Luxury and high-end**
- Tiffany & Co., Graff, Bulgari and Harry Winston: top-quality stones, with much higher prices.
- Auction houses and estate jewelers, such as 1stDibs, Christie's and Sotheby's: vintage and antique pieces. A classic oval sapphire with a diamond halo (the "Princess Diana" or Kate Middleton ring style) is often available this way.

**In India**
- Tanishq, Caratlane, Bluestone, Malabar Gold and local jewelers. Many carry neelam (blue sapphire) pendants, and some will make one to order.

**What to check before buying**
- Natural versus lab-created: lab sapphires cost much less. Natural stones should come with a lab report (GIA, IGI or similar).
- Treatment: most natural sapphires are heat-treated, which is normal. It should be disclosed.
- Metal: 14k or 18k white gold, or platinum, suits the blue and white look.
- Size: ask for carat weight and dimensions, because oval sapphire sizes vary widely.
- Return policy: look for at least 30 days.
