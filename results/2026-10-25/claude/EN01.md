Where to buy a 1 carat diamond ring in the USA (from general knowledge; I did not check live prices or inventory):

**Online (usually the best value and selection)**
- Blue Nile: large inventory, good diamond search filters, reliable grading.
- James Allen: 360° HD diamond videos, so you can inspect the actual stone.
- Brilliant Earth: ethical sourcing focus, lab-grown and natural options, good designs.
- Whiteflash: known for "Ideal Cut" stones, such as their A CUT ABOVE line. A good pick if you care most about sparkle.
- Ritani and Clean Origin: good for custom settings and lab-grown stones.

**In-person chains**
- Tiffany & Co., Cartier: premium brand pricing and luxury service.
- Signet brands (Kay, Zales, Jared): widely available with frequent sales. Prices are higher and the stones are often lower quality than online.
- Costco: surprisingly good prices on certified diamonds, but you need a membership.

**Other options**
- Local independent jewelers: custom work and personal service, and you can compare stones in person.
- Diamond District (47th St, New York) or downtown Los Angeles: wholesale-style prices, but negotiate carefully and verify credentials.

**Buying tips**
- Insist on a GIA or AGS certificate. Other labs are often more lenient in grading.
- Prioritize cut (Excellent or Ideal), then choose color (G–I) and clarity (VS2–SI1) for the best value. Many flaws are not visible to the naked eye.
- Rough price ranges: a natural 1 carat stone is about $4,000–$9,000 or more depending on quality. A lab-grown 1 carat stone is usually about $500–$1,500. The setting costs extra.
- Check the return policy (30 days or more) and ask for insurance and resizing details.
- Compare the same specifications across 2–3 sellers.
