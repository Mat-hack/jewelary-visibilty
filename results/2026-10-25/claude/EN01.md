Where to buy a 1 carat diamond ring:

**Online retailers (usually best value, large selection, certified stones)**
- Blue Nile: big selection of certified diamonds, and you can build your own ring.
- James Allen: 360-degree video of each stone, and you can build your own ring.
- Brilliant Earth: known for ethical and conflict-free sourcing and for lab-grown options.
- Whiteflash / Ritani / Clean Origin: Whiteflash is known for well-cut stones. Clean Origin specializes in lab-grown diamonds.

**Big retail chains (in-store, easier to return or service)**
- Tiffany & Co., Cartier and similar luxury houses: highest prices, strong brand premium.
- Kay, Zales, Jared, Signet brands: frequent promotions, but markups are often higher.
- Costco: often good prices on certified diamonds, if you're a member.

**Local independent jewelers**: you can see the stone in person, negotiate, and get custom work and resizing.

**Other options**
- Lab-grown diamonds: chemically identical to mined diamonds and typically 60-80% cheaper. A 1 carat lab-grown ring often costs a few hundred to a couple thousand dollars. A mined one is typically about $4,000-$8,000 or more, depending on quality.
- Estate, vintage or resale (e.g., 1stDibs, local estate dealers): can be cheaper.

**Buying tips**
- Insist on a grading report from GIA or AGS. IGI is common for lab-grown stones.
- Prioritize cut quality first (Excellent or Ideal), then color (G-I is a good value range) and clarity (VS2-SI1 is usually eye-clean).
- Compare prices across several sellers and check the return policy (30 days or more is common).
- Ask about insurance and resizing.
