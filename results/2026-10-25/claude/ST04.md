I didn't search the web for this. These are well-known places to buy a London blue topaz ring, from general knowledge.

**Online retailers**
- Blue Nile, James Allen and Brilliant Earth: good for fine jewelry in gold or platinum, usually with detailed stone specs.
- Amazon and Etsy: wide price range, including handmade and custom pieces. On Etsy, check seller reviews and ask for a stone treatment disclosure.
- Gemporia and Ross-Simons: gemstone-focused with frequent sales. Both are mainly in the UK and US.
- Jewelry Television (JTV): lots of topaz in sterling silver.
- Local independent jewelers: ask them to source or custom-make a ring.

**What to check before you buy**
- **Treatment:** nearly all London blue topaz is irradiated and heat-treated. This is normal and stable, but the seller should disclose it.
- **Color:** London blue is a deep, slightly grayish teal-blue. It is darker than Sky blue and Swiss blue, so compare photos and look for good saturation.
- **Cut and clarity:** topaz is usually eye-clean. Look for a good cut, because it affects sparkle.
- **Metal:** sterling silver is cheapest, and 14k gold is common for better rings. Topaz is hard (8 on the Mohs scale) but can chip if hit hard.
- **Returns and certification:** choose a seller with a return policy, and for expensive stones ask for a gem lab report.

**Typical prices**
Sterling silver rings often run about $30 to $150. Gold rings often cost a few hundred dollars and up, depending on stone size and design.
