**Online retailers where you can pick the gemstone and its quality**

- **Blue Nile** (US): You choose the setting first, then a loose stone. You can filter by cut, color, clarity and carat. It mainly sells diamonds, and its colored gemstone selection is smaller.
- **James Allen** (US): Sells diamonds and some colored gemstones. It has 360° video of each stone. You can filter by grade and see the stone before you set it in a ring.
- **Brilliant Earth** (US): Sells sapphires, emeralds, rubies and other stones. Its sapphires are sold mainly through a "build your own ring" tool. It also offers lab-grown stones.
- **Ritani and With Clarity**: Both let you build a ring around a chosen stone.
- **GemsNY and Gemporia**: Sell loose gemstones and settings. Their listings usually state treatment and origin.
- **Etsy**: Many independent jewelers will make a custom ring. Check seller reviews and ask for a lab report.
- **Local custom jewelers**: A local jeweler can show you stones in person and set the one you choose. This is often the best way to compare color and clarity yourself.

**What to look for when choosing quality**

- **Grading factors**: Color, clarity, cut and carat weight. For colored stones, color is usually the most important factor.
- **Treatment disclosure**: Many gemstones are heated or otherwise treated. Ask what treatment a stone has had, because it affects value.
- **Origin**: Natural or lab-grown, and the country of origin where it matters.
- **Lab report**: A certificate from an independent lab such as GIA, AGL, GRS or SSEF is worth asking for on higher-value stones.
- **Return policy**: Look for a return window of 30 days or more.
