Ruby is the traditional 40th anniversary gem, so jewelry is a good choice. These are US options, grouped by budget and type. I did not check current prices or stock, so confirm those on each site.

**Major jewelers (online and in-store)**
- **Blue Nile** (bluenile.com): large online selection of ruby rings, pendants, earrings and bracelets, with good quality information and returns.
- **Brilliant Earth** (brilliantearth.com): ruby rings and earrings, with an emphasis on ethically sourced stones.
- **James Allen** (jamesallen.com): good for loose rubies and custom settings, with 360-degree views of each stone.
- **Kay, Zales, Jared and Helzberg Diamonds**: mall and online chains with ruby jewelry at mid-range prices, plus frequent sales and in-person help.
- **Tiffany & Co., Cartier, Bulgari and Harry Winston**: luxury options if the budget is high.

**Mid-range and online marketplaces**
- **Amazon, Macy's, Nordstrom and Costco**: lab-created and natural ruby pieces at lower prices. Costco often has good value on fine jewelry.
- **Etsy**: handmade and vintage ruby pieces, and engraving or personalization. Check seller reviews first.
- **Ross-Simons** (ross-simons.com): affordable ruby jewelry in sterling silver and gold, often discounted.
- **Gemvara or Angara** (angara.com): customizable ruby settings. Angara specializes in colored gemstones.

**Unique or vintage**
- **1stDibs, Ruby Lane and local estate or antique jewelers**: vintage and antique ruby pieces, which can suit a 40-year milestone.
- **Independent local jewelers**: they can design a custom piece, or reset a ruby the couple already owns.

**Tips**
- **Gift ideas beyond a ring:** a ruby pendant, stud earrings, a bracelet, a tennis necklace, or a ruby-and-diamond band for a vow renewal.
- **Ruby quality:** look for a deep red color ("pigeon's blood" is the most prized), good clarity, and a certificate from GIA or AGL for expensive stones.
- **Lab-grown or natural:** lab-created rubies look the same at a much lower price. Natural rubies cost more, and heat treatment is common and should be disclosed.
- **Buying safety:** check the return policy and ask for an appraisal or certificate.
- **Personalization:** engraving with the wedding date or "40 years" adds meaning.

A budget and a recipient (wife, husband or both) would let me narrow this down to specific products.
