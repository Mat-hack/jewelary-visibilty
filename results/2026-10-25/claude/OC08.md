Ruby is the traditional 40th anniversary gem and colour. Places to buy:

**Jewellery (the classic choice)**
- Established jewellers such as Tanishq, Kalyan, Malabar or local jewellers in India. Internationally, Pandora, Signet brands (Kay, Zales), Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth, James Allen, and department stores like Macy's or John Lewis.
- Good options are a ruby pendant, ring, earrings or a bracelet. Natural rubies cost more. Lab-created rubies look nearly identical for much less.
- Ask for a certificate or gemstone report for any natural ruby.

**Custom or handmade**
- Etsy and independent jewellers can engrave or personalise a piece, for example with the wedding date or both names. Order 2 to 4 weeks ahead.

**Lower-cost ruby-themed gifts**
- Ruby-red items from Amazon, Etsy, Not On The High Street (UK) or Personalised Gifts shops, such as engraved frames, wine glasses, a "40 years" keepsake, a photo book, or a ruby-red rose bouquet.
- A bottle of red wine or a ruby port, with a personalised label, also fits the theme.

**Tips**
