Listed prices range from about **$219 to $10,000+**, depending on the ring’s metal, gemstone, and design. [Source](https://www.angara.com/c/london%2Bblue%2Btopaz-rings)

## What to check before buying

- **Confirm the shade:** Look for the exact term *London blue topaz*. It describes a deep, inky blue, darker than sky blue topaz.
- **Check the metal:** Sterling silver is usually the more affordable choice; solid 10K or 14K gold is a good option if you want fine jewelry.
- **Compare the details:** Check the gemstone’s carat weight, whether diamonds are included, and whether the listed weight refers to all stones combined.
- **Review the purchase terms:** Confirm your US ring size, delivery estimate, resizing costs, and return policy.

**My recommendation:** Start with Macy’s if you want to keep the price down, REEDS if you like the yellow-gold design, and Angara if you want more control over the setting and metal. Check current prices and stock on each listing before ordering.
