Question: "What kinds of London blue topaz rings can I buy in the USA, and what do they typically cost?"
web_search_used: yes

There are several styles and price ranges to consider:

- **Budget-friendly sterling silver:** Often roughly $35–$200, especially at retailers such as [JTV](https://www.jtv.com/category/jewelry/london-blue-topaz-rings/_/N-3508288548). Check whether the setting is solid sterling silver or silver with a gold-tone finish.
- **Statement rings:** Larger stones, cocktail settings, and halo designs can run from around $150 to several hundred dollars. [Macy’s](https://www.macys.com/shop/jewelry-watches/jewelry/rings/Stone/Blue%20Topaz?id=69905) carries both silver and gold options.
- **Solid gold solitaire rings:** Often several hundred dollars to over $1,000, depending on the gold, stone size, and design. [Angara](https://www.angara.com/c/london%2Bblue%2Btopaz-rings) lists London blue topaz solitaire styles starting around $649.
- **Gold and diamond rings:** Typically more expensive; fine-jewelry examples at [Bloomingdale’s](https://www.bloomingdales.com/buy/london-blue-topaz-ring) can cost over $1,000.
- **Engagement or alternative bridal rings:** Solitaire, bypass, and three-stone designs are available from specialist sellers such as [LUO Jewelry](https://www.luojewelry.com/collections/london-blue-topaz-rings).

These are approximate shopping ranges, not guarantees. Compare the metal purity, stone dimensions and carat weight, return policy, and total price before buying.
