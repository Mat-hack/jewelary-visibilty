## What to check before buying

- **Make sure it’s London blue:** London blue topaz is the deeper, darker blue variety. Some listings combine it with lighter sky-blue topaz, so check the stone description carefully.
- **Compare the metal:** Sterling silver is generally more affordable; solid 10K or 14K gold is a good option for fine jewelry.
- **Check the stone details:** Confirm the topaz carat weight, whether diamonds are included, and whether a stated carat weight covers all stones combined.
- **Review the terms:** Check your US ring size, shipping, resizing costs, warranty, and return policy before ordering.

**My recommendation:** If you want to spend under $100, start with BAUTLR. For more styles and customizable settings, look at Angara. If you specifically want solid 14K gold, compare Diamond & Sapphire with Bloomingdale’s.

Prices and availability can change, so verify the current listing before purchasing.
