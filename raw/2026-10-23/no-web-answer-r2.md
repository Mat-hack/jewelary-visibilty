Question: "Where can I buy a London blue topaz ring? I'm shopping in the USA."
web_search_used: no

You can look for London blue topaz rings at major jewelry retailers, department stores, and online gemstone specialists. Retailers such as Macy’s, JTV, and Angara are places to start, and local independent jewelers may also be able to source one.

If you're shopping on a budget, sterling silver settings are usually less expensive than solid gold. For a fine-jewelry piece, compare 14K gold settings and designs with or without diamond accents.

Make sure the product description specifically says “London blue topaz,” because “blue topaz” can also refer to lighter shades. Check the ring size, metal details, return policy, and final price before ordering.
