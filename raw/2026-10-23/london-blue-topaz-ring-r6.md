## What to check before buying

- **Stone color:** Make sure the listing specifically says *London blue topaz*. It is the deeper, darker blue variety, rather than the lighter sky-blue or Swiss-blue topaz.
- **Metal:** Sterling silver is generally more affordable; solid 10K or 14K gold is worth considering for a fine-jewelry piece.
- **Stone details:** Check the topaz carat weight, whether diamonds are included, and whether the stated weight covers the entire ring’s stones.
- **Practical details:** Confirm your ring size, US shipping options, resizing costs, and return policy.

**My starting recommendation:** Compare the REEDS ring around $400 with Macy’s sterling-silver option around $268. If you specifically want solid gold, start with Bloomingdale’s and compare the metal, stone size, and final sale price.
