Question: "What kinds of London blue topaz rings can I buy in the USA, and what do they typically cost?"

In the USA, London blue topaz rings are available in several common styles and price ranges. Prices vary with the setting metal, stone size, and any diamond accents:

- **Simple sterling silver rings:** Often around $40–$200. Browse [JTV’s London blue topaz rings](https://www.jtv.com/category/jewelry/london-blue-topaz-rings/_/N-3508288548).
- **Larger statement or halo rings:** Commonly around $150–$500 or more, especially for large stones or decorative settings. [Macy’s](https://www.macys.com/shop/jewelry-watches/jewelry/rings/Stone/Blue%20Topaz?id=69905) has both sterling silver and fine-jewelry options.
- **Solid gold solitaire rings:** Usually several hundred dollars and up. Explore [Angara’s London blue topaz collection](https://www.angara.com/c/london%2Bblue%2Btopaz-jewelry).
- **Gold rings with diamonds:** Often $1,000 or more, depending on gold purity, diamond weight, and design. Browse [Bloomingdale’s London blue topaz rings](https://www.bloomingdales.com/buy/london-blue-topaz-ring).
- **Everyday and alternative styles:** Bezel-set solitaires, three-stone rings, bypass bands, and floral clusters are available from specialist sellers such as [Peora](https://peora.com/collections/london-blue-topaz).

These ranges are guides rather than fixed prices, and sale prices can change. Check whether the setting is solid gold, gold-plated, or gold-over-silver, and confirm the product says “London blue topaz” rather than simply “blue topaz.”
